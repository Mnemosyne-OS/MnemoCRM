# MnemoCRM

A Mnemosyne OS cartridge. It is the customer book of a small business, a
freelancer or a craftsperson.

## What it does

- Keeps your clients, people or companies. Each client has a sheet with
  everything that happened, in order.
- Follows your deals: a request, a quote, an order. You name the steps
  yourself.
- Keeps a catalogue. A product is either made in series, with a stock, or a
  unique piece, which is available, reserved or sold.
- Notes your sales: in the workshop, online, or at a market. At a market the
  screen has large buttons and works without a network.
- Puts your follow-ups in the Mnemosyne OS agenda, so they ring. A client
  who asked not to be contacted is never followed up.
- Links a note or a dictation to a client's sheet, and Mnemosyne remembers
  it.
- Imports your existing client list from a CSV or Excel file.

## What it does not do

MnemoCRM notes a sale. It takes no payment and it issues no invoice. It is
not a certified cash register: if your business charges VAT, the sales screen
says so.

## Free, and what needs a licence

The cartridge is free up to 50 clients and 30 products for sale. Importing a
PDF catalogue, and going past those numbers, needs a Mnemosyne OS licence.
A book that is already bigger always opens in full: the limit applies to
creating, never to reading.

## Install

In Mnemosyne OS, open the MnemoHub, choose « Add a cartridge », and paste this
repository's URL: `https://github.com/Mnemosyne-OS/MnemoCRM`.

## Where your files go

Your clients, deals and sales are files in a folder you choose on your
machine, one file per record. What you ask Mnemosyne to remember goes into
the cartridge's own vault.
